# Code of Conduct

This is a **procedure**, not a promise. It names who handles reports, how fast, and what
happens next.

## Scope

Applies to every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, mailing lists — and
to project-related conduct elsewhere when it affects someone's safety or ability to
participate here.

## Expected

- Assume good faith and argue about the work, not the person.
- Accept that "no" is a normal answer, and give reasons when you say it.
- Respect stated limits: someone's time, their pronouns, their choice not to continue a thread.
- Credit others' work.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, demeaning remarks, and attacks based on any personal characteristic.
- Publishing private information without consent, including a reporter's identity.
- Sustained disruption: derailing threads, bad-faith argument, brigading, deliberate
  time-wasting.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports or assists a report.

## Reporting

Contact **{{COC_CONTACT_1}}**. If your report concerns that person, contact
**{{COC_CONTACT_2}}** instead. Reports from non-contributors are accepted.

Useful in a report: what happened, where (links), when, who was involved, and whether you want
any particular outcome. Partial reports are still worth sending.

## What happens

1. **Acknowledgement within {{COC_ACK_HOURS}} hours.**
2. Handlers with a conflict of interest — subject, reporter, close collaborator, or employed by
   an involved party — recuse themselves.
3. We review the report and the linked evidence, and may contact you for clarification. The
   subject is told what conduct is at issue; **your identity is not disclosed to them without
   your consent**.
4. A decision is communicated to the reporter and the subject within {{COC_DECISION_DAYS}} days,
   with the reason and any conditions.

Your identity is shared only among non-recused handlers, except where disclosure is legally
required — we will tell you if that happens.

## Consequences

| Level | Action | Who may impose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Private correction explaining the problem | any handler |
| 2 | Written warning with stated conditions and a review date | any handler |
| 3 | Temporary limits: thread ban, or suspension of participation, **with an end date** | {{COC_LEVEL3_AUTHORITY}} |
| 4 | Permanent removal from project spaces | {{COC_LEVEL4_AUTHORITY}} |

Level is chosen on severity and pattern, not on how important the person is to the project.

## Appeals

Levels 2–4 may be appealed within 30 days to {{COC_APPEAL_CONTACT}}, who must not have decided
the original case. One appeal per decision; the appeal outcome is final.

## Records

Reports and decisions are kept by the handlers for {{COC_RETENTION}} and are readable only by
non-recused handlers. They are kept so patterns are visible across time, and deleted on
schedule. Where GDPR applies, this is our stated storage-limitation period (Art. 5(1)(e)).

## Transparency

We publish aggregate counts of reports and actions once a year, with no identifying detail.

## Limits of this document

It does not replace law enforcement, does not create a legal duty, and cannot resolve technical
disagreements — those follow the escalation path in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
